## Public rules for WaveWarZ pieces

These came with the finals data pack, and they still apply:

- No wallet addresses.
- No trader named. That includes the buyer of any candle.
- No individual profit or loss, in SOL or dollars, for anyone.
- Nothing about any artist's health, how they felt after a result, or leaving a space.
- No work-day times: no "this morning", no "today at". "Sunday's finals" is fine. Show
  schedules the host announced, such as "Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 9 PM EST", are
  fine.
- No emojis, no hashtags, no em dashes. Use hyphens only.
- Numbers exactly as measured. Anything the host said on air is attributed to Hurricane.
- Tag only @wavewarz and @igniteradio_xyz. Use names for everyone else.
