## Draft, verbatim

ZM.

Sunday's finals came down to three factors, fifteen-minute chart clocks, and a one-second candle that flipped the entire room.

The format on @wavewarz is simple on paper. Two artists face off across three rounds. Each round is decided by the best of three factors: a seven-minute pinned X poll, a panel of three judges scoring 1 to 10 per track, and the live bonding curve on wavewarz.com where anyone buys or sells while the songs play. Whichever artist holds more SOL when the clock hits zero takes the chart factor. Best two of three rounds takes the tournament.

I tracked the charts against the Solana blockchain for the whole event. Every pool number Hurricane read out on air matched what the program held on chain to four decimals, and his countdown calls landed within three seconds of the on-chain bells. The play-by-play was a real record of the market.

Round one paired Geek Myth's psychedelic synth track "Brain on Shuffle" against Stormi's rock piece "Into the Maelstrom." Early trading had Geek at 1.2828 SOL to Stormi's 0.98. The lead swung four times inside the final two minutes: Stormi led 1.28 to 1.24 with 46 seconds left, trading froze for thirty seconds, then a sell on Stormi and a last-second buy on Geek secured Geek the chart factor. But the crowd and the panel went the other way. Stormi took the X poll with 34 votes, and the judges swept for her. Money Miller gave it to Stormi by a quarter point, Retrospect had it eight to seven for her storytelling, and Mansa scored it six to three, praising her stutter effect. Round one to Stormi.

Round two shifted the momentum. Stormi led off with "I Can Speak for Myself," followed by Geek Myth's comic hustler record "I Know a Guy." A massive sell hit Stormi's curve right at the end of her track, dropping her to 0.9747 SOL against Geek's 1.5515. Stormi fought back to 1.43 against 1.53, and briefly touched 1.53 against 1.6611 with 3:08 left. Geek pushed ahead to 1.91 after a buy at 48 seconds, and another massive sell hit Stormi at the final bell. Hurricane remarked after that somebody was rugging her chart. Geek swept round two cleanly: he took the chart factor, won the X poll with 44 votes, and earned the judges' factor after Mansa broke a three-three tie for Geek's concept and Money Miller backed him by a quarter point. One round each.

Round three was the rubber match. Attendance climbed to 70 in the Space. Geek played "Fall Through Me," which Retrospect likened to the WipEout soundtrack and Money Miller connected to classic D.C. raves. Stormi countered with "Persephone." Stormi held the trading lead for most of the fifteen minutes, sitting at 1.2850 SOL to Geek's 1.13 with 4:17 left. Geek recaptured the lead at 1.48 with 34 seconds left, but a sell at 14 seconds put Stormi back ahead. Then, right as another sell hit Stormi, a single buy landed on Geek Myth's side in the final second. On chain, his pool jumped from 1.4812 to 9.1213 SOL. As Hurricane put it, the god candle arrived.

Geek secured the chart factor with that move, locked in the judges with nods from Mansa and Retrospect, and won the poll with 54 votes. Hurricane announced Geek Myth the tournament champion, two rounds to one.

Geek closed out the stream with "Toast to WWZ" with his cat Butters on his lap. Regular weekday quick battles continue Monday, Wednesday, and Friday at 9 PM EST with DJ Wavy, morning spaces run weekdays at 11 AM EST with @igniteradio_xyz, and the gauntlet round between Replicant and Stilo comes next.
