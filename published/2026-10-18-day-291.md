## How it works

We meet in one room, then split into groups of up to 6.

Each person shares what they did for The ZAO that week.

Each group ranks the week's contributions by consensus. 1st place earns 110 Respect, then 68, 42, 26, 16 and 10.

I propose the results afterwards. They settle after a 72 hour vote and a 72 hour veto window.

Respect is earned, not bought. It is a record of contribution, and you cannot buy or sell it.

## How far this has come

We started recording fractal results in public on July 30, 2024. As of October 12, results were recorded in 72 different weeks, and 165 different people hold Respect. In July that number was 157.

The format comes from Eden Fractal and the fractal democracy work Dan Larimer started in 2021. We adapted it for The ZAO, an impact network bringing profit margin, data, and IP rights back to artists. Music first, community second, technology third.
