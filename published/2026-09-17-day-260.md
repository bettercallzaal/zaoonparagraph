Tom Fellenz is playing ZAOstock.

ZAOstock is a free music festival on Saturday, October 3, on the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine. 1 stage, 8 acts, from 12 to 6. At 6 the street clears, and Black Moon Public House next door hosts its own evening from 6. It is part of the 9th annual Art of Ellsworth, during Maine Craft Weekend.

Artists are published one at a time after confirming in writing and sending a bio and photo.

Now he is the third act we are publishing for Ellsworth.

A dynamic acoustic guitar instrumentalist, Tom has broad performance experience across event stages, clubs, cafes, and house concerts. His sets showcase a blend of influences, with his original music styled by 70s and 80s progressive rock, smooth jazz, and acoustic folk.

His playing style has a cinematic quality, having listeners say his music takes them on immersive journeys, from introspective motivation to making your mark in the world; all from 1 guitar. His set runs around 1 hour of original music.

Genre: Rock guitar and soundtrack

He closes the outdoor block, the last thing heard before the street clears.
