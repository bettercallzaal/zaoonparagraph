# Year of the ZABAL - Day 265: bounty two is open until 5 pm

Make one piece of media for ZAOstock with the brand kit. It closes at 5 pm Eastern today and I name my pick live on Twitch at the close.

zm

year of the zabal day 265

11 days until ZAOstock. Bounty two is open right now and it closes at 5 pm Eastern today.

## The ask

Make one piece of media for ZAOstock using the brand kit. Post it publicly, then claim it on the bounty with the link to your post. That is the whole thing.

This round I would most like to see video with sound. The kit has audio in it now, a 30-second spot and the 7 minute 39 radio interview from September 10, so there is something to cut to. Anything else is welcome too: a poster, a flyer, a story, a meme, a pitch to camera.

[Enter bounty two.](https://poidh.xyz/base/bounty/1410)

## What happens at 5

I name my pick live on Twitch right as it closes. Then it goes to everyone who added to the pot, and they vote to confirm it before it pays out. Nobody is paid on my word alone.

That is how an open bounty on poidh works. Someone posts an ask with a reward attached, anyone can answer it by doing the thing and posting the proof, anyone who wants the ask answered can add to the pot, and everyone who added gets a vote on the pick.

## How round one went

Six people entered. Four made the 4 pm close, and two came in after it, who are welcome to enter the same piece today.

My pick was a gig poster: the kit's orange cracked cement texture, the moose large and clean, and every fact a person needs on one image. That vote is open until Wednesday night. You can [see round one here.](https://poidh.xyz/base/bounty/1409)

## Everything you need

The [brand kit](https://zaostock.com/brand) has the moose by attabotty, and the signage, illustrations, colours and type by Candy of CandyToyBox, plus the audio. Please credit them when you use their work.

ZAOstock is a free music festival on Saturday, October 3, 2026, on the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine, from 12 to 6 pm Eastern. 8 acts on 1 stage, free for all ages. [See the running order.](https://zaostock.com/program) [RSVP for free.](https://ticket.zaostock.com)
