I love it when I can use my connector ability to show off other people's skill sets, and that's what's happening right now as I work with all these amazing builders.

## What WaveZStation is

WaveZStation is the WaveWarZ team's music store on Base. Instead of streaming a song, you buy into it: pay what you want, starting at $1 in USDC. Every sale splits three ways. 45% goes to the artist, 45% goes to the fans who already bought in, and 10% goes to the platform.

That middle 45% is the twist. Your cut of a song's fan pool follows how much you put in, so the earlier you back a song, the more you earn as other people buy after you. You can buy again any time to grow your share, and claim what you have earned from the song page.

## How it's going

WaveZStation went live on chain in late July. As of this afternoon the site lists 33 songs from 24 artists, about $2,459 bought in total, and 45% of every one of those sales went straight to the artist.

When we studied the contract last week, the typical song made most of its sales on its first day. That is why drop day matters so much, and why a song needs people talking about it after launch week too.
