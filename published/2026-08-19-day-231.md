**Three months of free workshops. 31 projects. 15 people who entered. It comes down to six.**

ZABAL Gamez Season 1 has its finalists. The finals are head-to-head battles on WaveWarZ, live-traded music battles. Anyone can watch. The market itself helps crown the champions.

This is what the season built. Here are the six carrying it to the finish.

# The six

## Artist track: n3m vs dee-13

![](https://img.paragraph.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=1280,height=853,format=webp/https://storage.googleapis.com/papyrus_images/0891fd815f86cd224b3502c9912be88d102c98fe031fe31c42fffe7a57a029e5.jpg)

n3m (LadyrynNemesis) arrives with N3M3SIS - THE CALL OUT, and did not stop at one lane: 3 published entries this season across all 3 tracks, including SURFBOARD on the builder board and a full ZABAL Gamez song and video.

dee-13 brings Ledger, a manga-style series about five art school friends who turn a talent for forgery into an underground operation. One masterpiece at a time.

## Builder track: jdwalka vs ghostmintops

![](https://img.paragraph.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=1280,height=853,format=webp/https://storage.googleapis.com/papyrus_images/a4c04b9e4348c70d62be5a63188a764d37e8709b81c00d6e1850aa5949ca1c59.jpg)

jdwalka put 3 projects on the board: Chroma Poker, a Farcaster poker app with a live HUD and hand-history parsing, a crypto prediction orchestrator wired to 22+ exchanges, and a running Farcaster Space teaching predictive apps to the community.

ghostmintops (Brandon) was the most prolific name of the season: 7 entries, 6 already published, across every track. Proof Drop lets builders prove what they shipped. The WaveWarZ Gravity Board reimagines the battle dashboard. There are original ZAO songs in there too. If a track existed, Brandon entered it.

## Creator track: uniquebeing404 vs presdency.eth

![](https://img.paragraph.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=1280,height=853,format=webp/https://storage.googleapis.com/papyrus_images/2ff89aa7a729720b27c96f714989e9b81354d85386e792876d2977549c82e0a3.jpg)

uniquebeing404 built ColorZAO, where you paint a grayscale canvas to reveal an independent artist's work underneath. Discovery through play instead of scrolling. It is **[live right now](https://colorzao.signalify.xyz/)** .

presdency.eth is building HOOD, an online market designed for both humans and AI agents to buy and sell.

No play-in. No wildcard. Shipping real work and staying active all season was the bar, and these six cleared it.
