Today I sit down with Gigi.

Gigi, aka GDUBBYZ, is a staple character of the WaveWarZ universe. She is one of our few non-musician music fans, and she brings an amazing energy while helping our friend Stilo World manifest some of his ideas, working on Lit Studio and Cyberfy.

## Who Gigi is

Gigi is a WaveWarZ listener who judges battles and gives artists feedback on their tracks. She came to WaveWarZ from mostly mainstream listening, and found it through Stilo after she joined X.

Her X bio describes her page as her little archive corner, where she also matches songs to art. It points people to [@LitStudio_io](https://x.com/LitStudio_io) and [@STILOWORLD](https://x.com/STILOWORLD).

In a ZAO community Space in August she said she has been supporting behind the scenes "because independent artists go through a lot".

## What she has been part of

Her first music battle ever was March 1, when Stilo faced ItzWonderfull from the VeVe community, a big moment for WaveWarZ. She joined Stilo's prep Spaces in the run-up, and that is where she first met and talked with Hurricane, Candy, Kata7yst and other community members, before she started joining WaveWarZ Spaces as a speaker.

She helped prep the community battle on Sunday, October 18, when WaveWarriorZ beat MB Wif HitMakerZ in all three rounds.

When ManWaveZ came out, Hurricane's song with Stilo on a beat by Benny J, Stilo and Hurricane both named her in the credits on air.
