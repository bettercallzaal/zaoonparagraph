## What this is

A three month build-a-thon run by The ZAO. Free, open to anyone, and not a generic hackathon: you build something real, in public, with a ZAO mentor alongside you the whole way.

June was the workshops - governance, Respect, fractals, and hands-on sessions on Claude Code, Cursor, MCP and agent harnesses. All recorded, all still up. July was the open build, where anyone could submit a project. August is the finals, and that is where we are now.

15 people entered across three tracks: artist, builder and creator. Everyone who entered is on the board. Six get picked as finalists, two from each track, and they present in the last week of August. Three win, one from each track.

## The board closes Sunday, August 16

That is the deadline. Until then nothing is decided, and everything can still move.

When it closes, I pick the six. All six present in three separate finals in the last week of August, and the six of you agree the dates between yourselves.

## Tonight at 5pm EST

On the zm stream I go through the board for the first time, live, and there is a special guest on after. Come and see where you stand.

## How the six get chosen

I choose them. It is not a tally that decides it for me.

There is no vote. A ballot ran back in month two, just to see what people thought of the projects. It did its job and it is closed now. It was never how the six get chosen.

What counts is showing up. Points go on the board at [zabalgamez.com/leaderboard](https://zabalgamez.com/leaderboard) for the things that actually move this forward: showing up to the zm stream at 5pm EST, taking on this week's challenge, shipping an update on your project. Everyone starts on a point for entering, and every score is public.
