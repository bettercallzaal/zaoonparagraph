Wednesday is builders day, and today the thing being built is this newsletter. If you got yesterday's email, you saw two extra sections sitting at the top. Here is what happened and what I changed.

## How I make this now

Every edition still starts with me and the facts: what happened in The ZAO, checked against the live numbers before anything gets written. Once the edition is done, I have been trying Paragraph's built-in agent to turn it into an X article and a LinkedIn post, so one edition can reach people wherever they already are.

That part works. The Day 299 and Day 300 X articles both came out of it.

## What broke

Yesterday morning I asked the agent to put the two social drafts at the top of our chat so I could see them. It put them at the top of the newsletter itself, which was already scheduled to send. Nothing checked the newsletter again after that, so it went to everyone with the X article and the LinkedIn post stuck on top.

The web version is fixed. The email could not be taken back.

## What I changed

Every scheduled edition now gets read again five minutes before it sends. If anything changed since I approved it, the send waits until I look. Social drafts now get made in their own chat, away from the newsletter.

This is an experiment, and I would love any and all feedback as we go. If something reads off, a format works better for you, or you want less or more of anything, tell me on Farcaster at @zaal or on X at @bettercallzaal.
