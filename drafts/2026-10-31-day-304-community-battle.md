## Two crews

On Sunday night, October 18, two crews met in a WaveWarZ battle: WaveWarriorZ against MB Wif HitMakerZ. About 14 SOL traded across three rounds, and WaveWarriorZ took all three.

## How it went

A round goes to whoever wins two of three votes: the X poll, the SOL vote on the trading charts, and the judges.

Round 1 finished 2.05 SOL to 1.60. The judges went with MB Wif HitMakerZ, but the poll and the charts went with WaveWarriorZ, so the round did too.

Round 2 is the one to look at. 8.57 SOL to 0.88, and all three votes went to WaveWarriorZ. That single round traded 9.46 SOL, the 11th biggest of the 1,674 battles on the public record.

Round 3 finished 0.55 to 0.35, and it split the same way as Round 1. The judges went with MB Wif HitMakerZ, the poll and the charts went with WaveWarriorZ.

So the judges sided with the losing crew in two rounds out of three, and it did not decide anything. That is how WaveWarZ is built. Judges count, and so does everyone listening.
