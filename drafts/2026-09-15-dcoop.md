ZAOstock is 19 days out, and from today we are introducing one musician a day until the street fills up on 3 October. DCoop goes first.

DCoop comes out of classic hip hop and refuses to stay there. Reggae, rock, punk, tribal, country, EDM and R&B all turn up in his records, a genre-defying sound that honors where it came from while it breaks new ground. A lot of his music is built on spiritual frequencies, 432 to 900 Hz, crafted to resonate rather than just play, and to heal.

He is out of the DMV, and he is @dcoopofficial on every platform.

He is also the reason the ZAO Festivals series already has a Maryland chapter. This July he co-hosted ZAOville, the pool party in Laurel, Maryland, as founder of The VEC, and played his own set that afternoon. ZAOville fed straight into ZAOstock, and now he is coming north to play it.

DCoop plays the afternoon block on the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine, on Saturday 3 October. Free to walk up to, all ages, rain or shine. One stage, eight acts, noon to six.
