LyonsDen is playing ZAOstock

ZAOstock is a free music festival on Saturday 3 October, on the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine. One stage, eight acts, noon to six. At six the street clears, and Black Moon Public House next door hosts its own evening from six. It is part of the 9th annual Art of Ellsworth, during Maine Craft Weekend.

LyonsDen is one of the eight.

LyonsDen Rez Muzik is a Native American, self-produced independent artist from the Onondaga Reservation. Reggae roots, hip hop, blues, R&B and EDM in one set, with Rez storytelling underneath it. He writes, records and produces his own music. After a run of singles and the EP "Tales From SouthEnd", he released his debut album, "The SouthEnd Kid".

He earned a spot at SXSW after winning a Battle of the Bands, then joined the Beyond The Block Tour. He has collaborated with artists in the Netherlands and Belgium.

We have worked with him before. He played ZAOville in Laurel, Maryland on 25 July, the pool party chapter of the ZAO Festivals series, the same day as DCoop. Now he is the second act we are publishing for Ellsworth.

His full bio, his genre and his music are on his page: https://zaostock.com/artist/lyonsden
