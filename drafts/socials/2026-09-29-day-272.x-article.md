Four days from now a street in downtown Ellsworth, Maine closes to traffic and becomes a stage. This is how to get to it.

By car, Ellsworth is about 40 minutes from Bangor, about 3 hours from Portland and about 5 hours from Boston. Route 1 and Route 1A run right through downtown. Franklin Street itself has no parking on the day, because it is the venue. The two free lots are the Franklin Street Parking Lot and the one at Ellsworth City Hall, both a short walk from the parklet.

By air, Bangor International is the nearest major airport, about 40 minutes away, with a direct shuttle bus to Ellsworth twice a day. Hancock County-Bar Harbor is a smaller regional airport about 20 minutes away.

If you are staying over, book now. Early October is foliage season at the gateway to Acadia National Park and rooms go fast. Our visitor page names 4 hotels, and the free Island Explorer shuttle connects Ellsworth-area hotels, Bar Harbor and Acadia through October 12.

ZAOstock is free and all ages, Saturday October 3, noon to 6 Eastern, Franklin Street Parklet. At 6 the street clears and Black Moon Public House next door hosts its own evening, with North Creek. Not in Maine? The whole outdoor block streams at zaostock.com/live.

See you on Franklin Street.
