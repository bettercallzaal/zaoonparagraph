## Draft, to post as an X Article

ZM.

Five days from now 8 independent acts play a closed street in downtown Ellsworth, Maine. It is the 4th ZAO Festivals event, and the first 3 are the reason it exists.

New York, April 2024. ZAO-PALOOZA ran with 12 artists, organised by volunteers in 6 weeks. 6 of the 12 had made music onchain before and 6 were completely new to it. There was no guest book: you signed a link of a paper chain and added it to the rest. It broke even, and that told us to do it again.

Miami, December 2024. ZAO-CHELLA took over a space in Wynwood during Art Basel week, with 16+ musicians and 100+ visual artists. WaveWarZ ran the first battle it had ever played live on a stage. A group of the artists stayed in a rented house with a mobile studio, and a song got made with about 10 of them on it. 3 people who could not make the trip made one of their own in about 30 hours, from different countries.

Maryland, July 2026. ZAOville was a pool party in Laurel, co-hosted with DCoop, founder of The VEC, with an open mic, live sets and a DJ night swim. DCoop and LyonsDen both played it, and both play ZAOstock.

Maine, October 3. ZAOstock is free and all ages, noon to 6 Eastern, on the Franklin Street Parklet. At 6 the street clears and Black Moon Public House next door hosts its own evening, with North Creek. Not in Maine? The whole outdoor block streams at zaostock.com/live.

Running order: https://zaostock.com/program
RSVP, free: https://ticket.zaostock.com

That is where it started. Come see where it goes.
