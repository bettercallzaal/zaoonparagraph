## Draft, to post as an X Article

ZM.

Three days from now 8 independent artists play a closed street in downtown Ellsworth, Maine. There are 3 ways to be there, and 2 of them do not need you to be in Maine.

The 1st is the real thing. Walk up to the Franklin Street Parklet any time from noon. It is free and all ages. There is no gate and no ticket is checked.

The 2nd is the stream. It plays at zaostock.com/live for the full window, noon to 6 Eastern. No sign-up, no account, no app. Bookmark the page now. If the picture stops on the day, the Telegram chat is where we say what is happening and when it is back.

The 3rd is a watch party. There is no single format. One channel carries clean event audio, and every host takes that audio and does their own thing over it, in their own room, for their own people. You can host one yourself, and nobody has to ask. Tell us it is happening and it goes on the list that goes out on the day.

The stream runs with the parklet. At 6 the street clears and Black Moon Public House next door hosts its own evening, with North Creek, and that part is in person only.

Watch: https://zaostock.com/live
Running order: https://zaostock.com/program
RSVP, free: https://ticket.zaostock.com

See you there.
