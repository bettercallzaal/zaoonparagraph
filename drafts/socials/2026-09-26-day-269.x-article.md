## Draft, to post as an X Article

ZM.

One week from today a street in downtown Ellsworth, Maine closes, a stage goes up on the Franklin Street Parklet, and eight independent acts play back to back from noon to 6. Today's edition, written by IMan, is the first time we have put the whole road to that street in one place. Here is the short version.

The first time most of us were in the same room was ZAO-PALOOZA, New York, 2024, during the conference week, with 12 artists on the bill. People who had only known each other as names and profile pictures met for real. It told us to do it again.

On December 6, 2024, in the middle of Art Basel week, ZAO-CHELLA took over a space in Wynwood, Miami. Doors at 4 for networking. At 6, WaveWarZ played the first battle it had ever run live on a stage, a rematch of one fought online. From 7 until the 11 pm close, 16+ musicians played, with 100+ visual artists, AR art and trading cards in the room. Music, art and a live battle on one stage, in a city packed with events. That is the energy we are carrying to Maine.

Between the big nights, COC Concertz kept the music going online, with concerts in the metaverse streamed live, so nobody needed a plane ticket to be part of it. Today's pre-party, with Thy Revolution and COC Concertz on the zaofestivals Twitch channel, was that same spirit one week before the flagship day. The replay is there if you missed 4 pm.

All of it leads to next Saturday. ZAOstock is free and all ages, October 3, noon to 6 Eastern on the Franklin Street Parklet, part of the 9th Annual Art of Ellsworth during Maine Craft Weekend. Eight acts in running order: The Crown Vics open, then OPEN X, Grass Rug, Acadia Rising, Michael Anderson, and the ZAO musicians who travel in, DCoop, LyonsDen and Tom Fellenz, closing the day. At 6 the street clears and Black Moon Public House next door hosts its own evening. Not in Maine? The whole outdoor block streams at zaostock.com/live.

We put this on a real street on purpose. Eat at the places around you, drink at Black Moon, buy something from the shop you walk past. We are measuring what October 3 does for this block, and that number only exists if you show up and make it.
