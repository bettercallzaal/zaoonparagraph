## Draft, to post on LinkedIn

Eight days out from ZAOstock, and the thing I am proudest of about the bill is the ratio.

Five of the eight acts playing are Maine artists. The Crown Vics open at noon, a rock and roll dance band that has worked rooms from Portland to Halifax. OPEN X play second, a three-piece whose recent dates run Ellsworth, Bar Harbor and Lucerne. Grass Rug play third, an indie jam rock band out of Portland. Acadia Rising play fourth, original flute and hand percussion, and they are from Ellsworth, where the festival is held. Michael Anderson plays fifth, from Bar Harbor, blues and soul and jazz on voice and keys. Three more artists travel in to close the day, from the DMV, from the Onondaga Reservation and from the San Francisco Bay Area.

That balance was a decision, not an accident. A free festival in a small city that imports all of its music is a visiting show. One that books only inside the county is a local show. Putting both on one stage in one afternoon is the reason to build it at all, and it is why the acts who travel are sharing a bill with the people who play these rooms every month.

ZAOstock is free and all ages, Saturday October 3, 2026, 12 to 6 pm Eastern on the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine, as part of the 9th Annual Art of Ellsworth during Maine Craft Weekend.
