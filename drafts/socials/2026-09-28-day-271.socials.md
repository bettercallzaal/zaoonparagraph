## 6. Facebook

ZM. Five days until ZAOstock, and today I want to tell you where it came from.

ZAOstock is the 4th event in a series called ZAO Festivals. The first was in New York City in April 2024, with 12 artists, organised by volunteers in 6 weeks. The second was in Miami that December, during Art Basel week, with 16+ musicians and 100+ visual artists. The third was a pool party in Laurel, Maryland this July. Every one of them has been free to attend.

The 4th is here. Saturday October 3, noon to 6, Franklin Street Parklet, downtown Ellsworth. Free and all ages. See you there: https://zaostock.com/program
