ZM.

Five days from now Grass Rug walk onto the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, third of eight acts at ZAOstock, and play a set nobody has heard before, including them.

They are a rock band from Portland, Maine, known for an indie jam-rock sound. The group formed in 2025 and there are five of them: Sam Mitchell on vocals and rhythm guitar, Jack Howianec on lead guitar, Jacob Mitchell on rhythm guitar, Donny Bowman on bass and Peter Coleman on drums.

They are heavily inspired by the psychedelic rock of the 60s and 70s, along with 90s alternative and 2010s indie. What they are known for is the live show. Their sets are improvisational, and they have been drawing audiences across southern, central and downeast Maine.

A jam band on an open street is a good fit. They play 3rd, early in the day, so come at noon.

ZAOstock is free and all ages, Saturday October 3, noon to 6 Eastern, Franklin Street Parklet, downtown Ellsworth, Maine, part of the 9th Annual Art of Ellsworth during Maine Craft Weekend. At 6 the street clears and Black Moon Public House next door hosts its own evening, with North Creek. Not in Maine? The whole outdoor block streams at zaostock.com/live.
