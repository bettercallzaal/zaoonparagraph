## Draft, to post on LinkedIn

ZM. Five days until ZAOstock, and today's edition is about the 3 events that made it possible.

ZAO Festivals started in New York City in April 2024. 12 artists, organised by volunteers in 6 weeks, and it broke even. That December we went to Miami during Art Basel week with 16+ musicians and 100+ visual artists, and WaveWarZ ran its first live battle on a stage. This July the third event was a pool party in Laurel, Maryland, co-hosted with DCoop.

The idea has not changed since the first one. In New York we put 6 artists who had made music onchain next to 6 who were completely new to it, on purpose. In Miami a group of artists shared a house with a mobile studio, and 3 people who could not make the trip made a song of their own in about 30 hours from different countries. You bring people together, and things get made that would not have been made.

The 4th is ZAOstock: Saturday October 3, noon to 6 pm Eastern, on the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine, free and all ages.

See you there.
