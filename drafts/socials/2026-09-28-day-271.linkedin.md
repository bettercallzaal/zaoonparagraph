ZM. Five days until ZAOstock, and today's edition is about one of the five Maine acts on the bill.

Grass Rug are a five piece rock band from Portland, Maine, formed in 2025: Sam Mitchell on vocals and rhythm guitar, Jack Howianec on lead guitar, Jacob Mitchell on rhythm guitar, Donny Bowman on bass and Peter Coleman on drums. They play indie jam rock, inspired by the psychedelic rock of the 60s and 70s along with 90s alternative and 2010s indie, and what they are known for is the live show. Their sets are improvisational, and they have been drawing audiences across southern, central and downeast Maine.

They play 3rd on a bill of eight, on a street that closes to traffic for the afternoon. A band that builds its set on the night, in the open air, in front of a crowd that walked in for free, is the kind of booking this festival exists for.

ZAOstock is Saturday October 3, noon to 6 pm Eastern, on the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine, free and all ages, part of the 9th Annual Art of Ellsworth during Maine Craft Weekend.
