## Draft, to post on LinkedIn

ZM. Three days until ZAOstock, and today's edition is about access.

The festival is free and on a public street, so the first way to be there is to walk up. No gate, no ticket checked. But most of the community that built this day does not live in Maine, so the second and third ways matter as much. The whole outdoor block streams for the full six hours, with no sign-up, no account and no app. And anyone can run a watch party: one channel carries clean event audio, and each host takes it into their own room for their own people.

Nobody has to ask permission to host. Tell us it is happening and it goes on the list that goes out on the day.

ZAOstock is Saturday October 3, noon to 6 pm Eastern, on the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine, free and all ages.

Watch: https://zaostock.com/live
RSVP, free: https://ticket.zaostock.com

See you there.
