## Draft, to post as an X Article

ZM.

Six days from now the last three acts at ZAOstock take the Franklin Street Parklet in turn, and between them they have come further than anyone else on the bill. Each has had an edition of their own. This is the three of them together, because the back half of the afternoon is worth planning your day around.

DCoop plays 6th. He is a hip-hop artist from the DMV and the founder of The VEC, and his music starts in classic hip-hop and pulls from reggae, rock, punk, tribal, country, EDM and R&B. A lot of it is built around frequencies between 432 and 900 Hz, made to resonate rather than just play. He co-hosted ZAOville with us in July and played his own set there.

LyonsDen plays 7th. LyonsDen Rez Muzik is a Native American, self-produced independent artist from the Onondaga Reservation, putting reggae roots, hip-hop, blues, R&B and EDM into one set with Rez storytelling underneath it. After the EP "Tales From SouthEnd" came his debut album, "The SouthEnd Kid". He won a Battle of the Bands to earn a spot at SXSW, joined the Beyond The Block Tour, and has collaborated with artists in the Netherlands and Belgium. He played ZAOville too.

Tom Fellenz plays 8th and closes the outdoor block. He is an acoustic guitar instrumentalist from the SF Bay Area, with original music shaped by 70s and 80s progressive rock, smooth jazz and acoustic folk. Listeners say his playing takes them on a journey, all from one guitar. His is the last thing you hear before the street clears.

Three artists from three places, on one stage in downtown Ellsworth, closing a day that opens with five Maine acts. At 6 the street clears and Black Moon Public House next door hosts its own evening, with North Creek.

ZAOstock is free and all ages, Saturday October 3, noon to 6 Eastern, Franklin Street Parklet, downtown Ellsworth, Maine, part of the 9th Annual Art of Ellsworth during Maine Craft Weekend. Not in Maine? The whole outdoor block streams at zaostock.com/live.
