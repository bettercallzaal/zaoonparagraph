## 6. Facebook

ZM. Eight days until ZAOstock, and today I wrote about the five Maine acts on the bill.

The Crown Vics open at noon, a rock and roll dance band from the Ellsworth and Bangor area. OPEN X play second. Grass Rug come up from Portland.
