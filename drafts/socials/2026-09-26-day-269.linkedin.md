## Draft, to post on LinkedIn

ZM. One week until ZAOstock, and today's edition is the first time the whole story is in one place.

ZAO Festivals started as a community that met online. The first time most of us were in the same room was ZAO-PALOOZA in New York in 2024, 12 artists on the bill. That December, ZAO-CHELLA took over a space in Wynwood during Art Basel week, with 16+ musicians, 100+ visual artists and a live music battle on stage. Between those nights, COC Concertz kept the music going with concerts streamed live, so nobody needed a plane ticket to be part of it.

Next Saturday, October 3, all of that lands on a real street: the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine, free and all ages, noon to 6, eight acts, five of them Maine artists, as part of the 9th Annual Art of Ellsworth during Maine Craft Weekend. We chose a street on purpose. The measure of the day is what it does for the businesses on that block, and that number only exists if people show up.

The edition was written by IMan, who has built the site, the artist pages and the stream page with us from Lusaka, six hours ahead of Ellsworth. The full story is in today's edition.
