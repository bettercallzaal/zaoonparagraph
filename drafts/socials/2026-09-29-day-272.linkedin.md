## Draft, to post on LinkedIn

ZM. Four days until ZAOstock, and today's edition is the practical one.

A free festival only works if people can get to it, so this one is about the trip. Ellsworth is about 40 minutes from Bangor, about 3 hours from Portland and about 5 hours from Boston. Bangor International is the nearest major airport, with a direct shuttle bus to Ellsworth twice a day. Franklin Street closes to traffic and becomes the venue, and the two free lots, the Franklin Street Parking Lot and the one at Ellsworth City Hall, are a short walk away.

If you are staying the night, book now. Early October is foliage season at the gateway to Acadia National Park and rooms go fast. The edition names the 4 hotels on our visitor page.

ZAOstock is Saturday October 3, noon to 6 pm Eastern, on the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine, free and all ages.

Plan the trip: https://zaostock.com/ellsworth
RSVP, free: https://ticket.zaostock.com

See you there.
