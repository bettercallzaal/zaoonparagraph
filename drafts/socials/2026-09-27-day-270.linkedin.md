## Draft, to post on LinkedIn

ZM. Six days until ZAOstock, and today's edition is about the three artists who travel furthest to close it.

DCoop comes in from the DMV, a hip-hop artist and the founder of The VEC, whose music is built around frequencies between 432 and 900 Hz and who co-hosted our ZAOville show in July. LyonsDen Rez Muzik comes in from the Onondaga Reservation, a self-produced artist who won a Battle of the Bands for a spot at SXSW and has collaborated with artists in the Netherlands and Belgium. Tom Fellenz comes in from the San Francisco Bay Area and closes the outdoor block on a single acoustic guitar.

They headline a bill where five of the eight acts are Maine artists. That is the shape of the day on purpose: a free festival in a small city that only imports its music is a visiting show, and one that only books locally is a local show, so we built it to be both, on one stage, in one afternoon. When the street clears at 6, Black Moon Public House next door hosts its own evening with North Creek.

ZAOstock is Saturday October 3, noon to 6 pm Eastern, on the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine, free and all ages, part of the 9th Annual Art of Ellsworth during Maine Craft Weekend.
