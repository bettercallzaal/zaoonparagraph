year of the zabal day 268

DCoop is playing ZAOstock, and he is coming from the DMV to do it.

ZAOstock is a free music festival on Saturday, October 3, on the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine. 1 stage, 8 acts, from 12 to 6. At 6 the street clears, and Black Moon Public House next door hosts its own evening from 6. It is part of Art of Ellsworth, during Maine Craft Weekend.

DCoop is one of the 8. He plays 6th, as the afternoon turns.

He comes out of classic hip hop and then refuses to stay there. His own list of what he blends into it runs reggae, rock, punk, tribal, country, EDM and R&B, which reads like a dare until you notice the phrase he uses for what he is doing with it: honouring tradition while breaking new ground.

There is one more thing in his bio that most artists would leave out. He builds much of his music on spiritual frequencies, 432 to 900 Hz, and he says it plainly: crafted to resonate deeply and promote conscious healing beyond conventional limits. That is his claim about his own work, and you can decide what you make of it standing in front of it on a Saturday afternoon.

A hip hop set with that range in it, outdoors, in the sixth slot, is the part of the day where the bill stops being a lineup and starts being a range.

He is @dcoopofficial on every platform, and his links are at [dot.cards/dcoope2](https://dot.cards/dcoope2).
