year of the zabal day 264

Michael Anderson is playing ZAOstock.

ZAOstock is a free music festival on Saturday, October 3, on the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine. 1 stage, 8 acts, from 12 to 6. At 6 the street clears, and Black Moon Public House next door hosts its own evening from 6. It is part of Art of Ellsworth, during Maine Craft Weekend.

Michael is one of the 8, and the 4th act we are publishing.

He lives in Bar Harbor, about 25 miles down the road from Ellsworth. His roots are in blues, soul and jazz, and he pairs an outstanding voice with clever keyboard playing. He is as comfortable performing jazz standards as popular hits, and he has played both as a solo act and in numerous bands. On his own he delivers a very full sound, which is what Franklin Street is getting.

In his words: "I find it very important to connect with my audience."

He describes himself as a student of jazz with a lifetime love of music. The first 3 artists we published are travelling to Maine from the DMV, the Onondaga Reservation and the SF Bay Area. Michael is the first one from Downeast Maine, and a festival in Ellsworth should sound like where it is as well as who is coming to it.

He plays 5th, solo piano, in the middle of the afternoon.
