Michael Anderson is playing ZAOstock.

ZAOstock is a free music festival on Saturday, October 3, on the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine. 1 stage, 8 acts, from 12 to 6. At 6 the street clears, and Black Moon Public House next door hosts its own evening from 6, with North Creek. It is the first independent music event of the 9th Annual Art of Ellsworth, Maine Craft Weekend.

Michael is one of the 8, and one of the 5 Maine acts on the bill.

He lives in Bar Harbor. His roots are in blues, soul and jazz, and he pairs an outstanding voice with clever keyboard playing. He is as comfortable performing jazz standards as popular hits, and he has played both as a solo act and in numerous bands. On his own he delivers a very full sound, which is what Franklin Street is getting.

In his words: "I find it very important to connect with my audience."

He describes himself as a student of jazz with a lifetime love of music. A festival in Ellsworth should sound like where it is as well as who is coming to it, and a Bar Harbor pianist is part of that.

He plays 5th, solo piano.

His page: [zaostock.com/artist/michael-anderson](https://zaostock.com/artist/michael-anderson)
