year of the zabal day 271

Grass Rug are playing ZAOstock.

ZAOstock is a free music festival on Saturday, October 3, on the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine. 1 stage, 8 acts, from 12 to 6. At 6 the street clears, and Black Moon Public House next door hosts its own evening from 6. It is part of Art of Ellsworth, during Maine Craft Weekend.

Grass Rug are one of the 8.

They are a rock band from Portland, Maine, known for an indie jam-rock sound. The group formed in 2025 and there are 5 of them: Sam Mitchell on vocals and rhythm guitar, Jack Howianec on lead guitar, Jacob Mitchell on rhythm guitar, Donny Bowman on bass and Peter Coleman on drums.

They are heavily inspired by the psychedelic rock of the 60s and 70s, along with 90s alternative and 2010s indie. What they are known for is the live show. Their sets are improvisational, and they have been drawing audiences across southern, central and downeast Maine.

A jam band on an open street is a good fit. They play 3rd, early in the day, so come at noon.
