## The bounty

Make one piece of media for ZAOstock using the brand kit. That is the whole ask. A poster, a clip, a story, a meme, a flyer, a 15-second pitch to camera, whatever you want. Post it publicly and claim it on the bounty.

## What poidh is

poidh stands for pics or it didn't happen. Someone posts an ask with a reward attached. Anyone can answer it by doing the thing and posting the proof. On a regular bounty the person who posted the ask picks the winner. On an open bounty, anyone who wants the ask answered can add to the pot, and everyone who added gets a vote on the winner.

That is the whole mechanism, and it is why we are using it.

## Why The ZAO is using it

Most promotion is paying a platform to put your post in front of people who did not ask for it. A bounty turns that around. Almost all of the money goes to a person, not a platform, and it goes to them for making something. The ask is public, the answers are public, and the pick happens live where anyone can watch. The people who paid for it get a vote.

For a free festival that is a better use of a promotion budget than an ad. Every entry is a real person making a real thing about ZAOstock and posting it to the people who follow them. And it is something any artist in The ZAO can do for their own release: put up an ask, fund it, and pay the people who show up for it rather than the feed.

So from today until ZAOstock there is one bounty a day, 13 in all, each closing at 4 pm Eastern with the pick named live at 5. This is day 1.
