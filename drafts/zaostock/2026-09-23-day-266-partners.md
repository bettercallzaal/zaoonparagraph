10 partners are behind ZAOstock.

ZAOstock is a free music festival on Saturday, October 3, on the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine. 1 stage, 8 acts, from 12 to 6. At 6 the street clears, and Black Moon Public House next door hosts its own evening from 6. It is part of Art of Ellsworth, during Maine Craft Weekend.

A free festival only happens if people give what they already have. Partners give time, venue and infrastructure. Nobody here paid to be listed, and every one of them has a named point of contact on the ZAO team.

The City of Ellsworth is the venue, the Franklin Street Parklet. Black Moon Public House, next door, is the evening and the official after-party. Wallace Events is the event equipment and the tenting, which is why this runs rain or shine. Star 97.7 is local radio promotion. Heart of Ellsworth is our community partner, through Art of Ellsworth and Maine Craft Weekend.

COC Concertz is co-presenter. WaveWarZ brings the live music-battle format it runs online all year. Bomb Squad is crew, content and merch. WE THE MEDIA is media and content capture. Artizen is the funding partner.

That is 10, and the page is at [zaostock.com/partners](https://zaostock.com/partners).

The other thing a free festival runs on is hands. There are 7 volunteer roles: setup, check-in, water, safety, teardown, content, and floater for anyone who wants to go where they are needed. Shifts run from morning load-in through evening teardown, and you can take 1 block or the whole day. You get a ZAOstock crew shirt and a meal, and there is no commitment until you say yes to a specific shift.

Sign up at [zaostock.com/volunteer](https://zaostock.com/volunteer).

RSVP is free at [ticket.zaostock.com](https://ticket.zaostock.com) so we know how many people to plan for. If you want to put something behind the day you can do that at [zaostock.com/tickets](https://zaostock.com/tickets).
