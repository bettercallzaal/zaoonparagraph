## 7. Reply kit

No clock time for any act: the site publishes the order, not times.

**Is it really free?** Yes. It is free to attend all day, with no gate and no wristband to buy. The $20 and $50 tiers fund the day and neither one gets you a better spot. (Source: zaostock.com/tickets)

**Where do I park?** There is a public parking lot plus free street parking right near the Franklin Street Parklet. You do not need a shuttle. (Source: zaostock.com/ellsworth)

**What if it rains?** It runs rain or shine, under tent cover from Wallace Events. Early October in Ellsworth has roughly a 1 in 3 chance of rain on any given day, so bring a rain layer. (Source: zaostock.com/program and zaostock.com/ellsworth)

**Who is playing, and in what order?** 8 acts, in this order: The Crown Vics, OPEN X, Grass Rug, Acadia Rising, Michael Anderson, DCoop, LyonsDen, Tom Fellenz. We publish the order, not set times, so come at noon to catch all of them. (Source: zaostock.com/program)

**Can I bring kids?** Yes, it is all ages and it is outdoors on a closed street. There are portable restrooms on site, including ADA-accessible units. (Source: zaostock.com/ and zaostock.com/ellsworth)

**Can I volunteer?** Yes. There are 7 roles, you get a crew shirt and a meal, and there is no commitment until you say yes to a specific shift: zaostock.com/apply (Source: zaostock.com/apply)

**How do I watch if I am not in Maine?** The stream plays at zaostock.com/live from 12 to 6 Eastern, with no sign-up. You can also join a watch party or host one, and the list of places to watch goes out on the day. (Source: zaostock.com/live)

**What does the $20 or the $50 do?** Both go to artist fees, materials and production, and both credit you as a supporter on the festival page. The $50 Pro Ticket has 20 spots and adds a 1:1 with someone on the ZAO team before the event. Payment today is by PayPal from the tickets page. (Source: zaostock.com/tickets)
