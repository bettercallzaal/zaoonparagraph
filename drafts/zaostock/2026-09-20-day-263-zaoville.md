year of the zabal day 263

ZAOstock is the 4th chapter. This is the 3rd.

ZAOstock is a free music festival on Saturday, October 3, on the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine. 1 stage, 8 acts, from 12 to 6. At 6 the street clears, and Black Moon Public House next door hosts its own evening from 6. It is part of Art of Ellsworth, during Maine Craft Weekend.

On Saturday 25 July we ran ZAOville, a pool party in Laurel, Maryland, co-hosted with DCoop, founder of The VEC. Free entry, free drinks. It opened with a vibe sesh and food, moved into an open mic, then live sets from independent artists: Ashley, DCoop, LyonsDen and Paper and Profit. A DJ night swim closed it. The VEC provided the sound, the wireless mics and the monitors.

2 of them are on the Ellsworth stage in 13 days. DCoop and LyonsDen both played Laurel, and both are coming north. That is the point of running these as a series. The people who play one chapter help build the next.

ZAO Festivals has 4 chapters now. ZAO-PALOOZA in New York City in April 2024, 12 artists, the community's first meetup in person, organised by volunteers in 6 weeks. ZAO-CHELLA in Miami in December 2024, in Wynwood during Art Basel, with 16+ musicians, 100+ visual artists and the first live WaveWarZ battle. ZAOville in July. And ZAOstock, the flagship, the first one in Maine.

Every one of them has been free to attend.
