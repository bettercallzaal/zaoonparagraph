year of the zabal day 268

LyonsDen is playing ZAOstock, and he is travelling from the Onondaga Reservation to do it.

ZAOstock is a free music festival on Saturday, October 3, on the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine. 1 stage, 8 acts, from 12 to 6. At 6 the street clears, and Black Moon Public House next door hosts its own evening from 6. It is part of Art of Ellsworth, during Maine Craft Weekend.

LyonsDen is one of the 8. He plays 7th, near the end of the day.

LyonsDen Rez Muzik is his own name for what he does: a Native American, self-produced independent artist carving out a lane where reggae roots, hip-hop grit, blues soul, R&B melodies and EDM energy collide. His own description of the result is the useful one, because it is two things at once. Uplifting island vibes and raw Rez storytelling. Healing and hard-hitting.

The catalogue behind that is real work rather than a promise. Multiple singles, then the EP "Tales From SouthEnd", then the debut album "The SouthEnd Kid". In his words the range runs from romantic anthems to humorous Rez punchline records to deeply reflective tracks about overcoming adversity, which is a wider span than most artists will admit to in a bio.

He describes it as a movement with a mission bigger than music, built through authentic storytelling and grassroots hustle. Seventh slot, outdoors, late in a free festival in Maine is exactly the kind of room that gets built that way.
