year of the zabal day 269

1 week until ZAOstock. Seven days from now a street in downtown Ellsworth, Maine closes, a stage goes up on the Franklin Street Parklet, and 8 independent acts play back to back from noon to 6. Today we want to tell you how we got here, and we want you with us when we do.

## The pre-party is today, 4 pm Eastern

Today, Saturday, September 26, at 4 pm Eastern, we are throwing the ZAOstock pre-party with Thy Revolution and COC Concertz. It streams on Twitch at [twitch.tv/zaofestivals.](https://www.twitch.tv/zaofestivals) That is the same channel that carries the whole of October 3. We are going live to talk through the whole story of ZAO Festivals: every event so far, what worked, what we learned, and how a community that met online ended up putting on a free festival on a street in Maine.

Bring questions. If you were at one of our events, bring your stories, because we want to hear them. If you have never been to one, this is the best hour to find out what you are walking into on October 3.

## Where it started: ZAO-PALOOZA, New York, 2024

The first time a lot of us were in the same room. ZAO-PALOOZA ran during NFT NYC in 2024 with 12 artists on the bill. People who had only known each other as names and profile pictures met for real, and the thing we had been building online turned into a night of live music.

It broke even. For a first event, run by a community with no venue of its own, that was a win, and it told us to do it again.

## Going bigger: ZAO-CHELLA, Miami, December 2024

On December 6, 2024, in the middle of Art Basel week, ZAO-CHELLA took over a space in Wynwood, Miami. Doors opened at 4 pm for networking. At 6, WaveWarZ played the first battle it had ever run live on a stage, a rematch of one fought online. From 7 until the 11 pm close, 16+ musicians played, with 100+ visual artists, AR art and trading cards in the room.

Music, art and a live battle on the same stage, in a city packed with events. That is the energy we are carrying to Maine.

## Between the big nights: COC Concertz

When we were not in the same city, COC Concertz kept the music going online, with concerts in the metaverse streamed on Twitch, so nobody needed a plane ticket to be part of it. Today's pre-party is that same spirit, one week before our flagship day.

## Where it is going: ZAOstock, October 3

All of that leads to next Saturday. ZAOstock is a free music festival on Saturday, October 3, on the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine, part of the 9th Annual Art of Ellsworth during Maine Craft Weekend. 1 stage, 8 acts, noon to 6, no ticket and no wristband for the street.

Here is who you will hear, in running order. The Crown Vics open the day, a rock n roll dance band. OPEN X bring power pop rock. Grass Rug bring indie jam rock from Portland, Maine. Acadia Rising bring world rhythms, flute and hand percussion, from Ellsworth itself. Michael Anderson plays voice and keys, blues, soul and jazz, from Bar Harbor. DCoop brings hip-hop that pulls from reggae, rock, punk, tribal, country, EDM and R&B. LyonsDen brings reggae roots, hip-hop grit, blues soul, R&B and EDM. Tom Fellenz closes the outdoor block on solo instrumental acoustic guitar.

At 6 the street clears and the after-party moves next door to Black Moon Public House, with a DJ.

Not in Maine? The whole outdoor block streams live at zaostock.com/live, noon to 6 Eastern on October 3.

## Why it is on Franklin Street

We put this on a real street on purpose. Eat at the places around you, drink at Black Moon, buy something from the shop you walk past. We are measuring what October 3 does for this block, and that number only exists if you show up and make it.

## A word from IMan

I have spent these last weeks building ZAOstock with Zaal from Lusaka, Zambia, 6 hours ahead of Ellsworth: the site, the artist pages and the stream page you will be watching. Seeing it come together from the other side of the world has been something else. Come to the pre-party today, say hi, and then let's make October 3 loud.

Join us at 4 pm today to hear where it started. Then come see where it goes.
