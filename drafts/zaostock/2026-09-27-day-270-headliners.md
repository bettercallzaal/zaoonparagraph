year of the zabal day 270

DCoop, LyonsDen and Tom Fellenz headline ZAOstock. They are the last 3 acts of the day.

ZAOstock is a free music festival on Saturday, October 3, on the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine. 1 stage, 8 acts, from 12 to 6. At 6 the street clears, and Black Moon Public House next door hosts its own evening from 6. It is part of the 9th Annual Art of Ellsworth, during Maine Craft Weekend.

Each of them has had an edition of their own. This is the 3 of them together, because the back half of the afternoon is worth planning your day around.

DCoop plays 6th. He is a hip-hop artist from the DMV and the founder of The VEC, and his music starts in classic hip-hop and pulls from reggae, rock, punk, tribal, country, EDM and R&B. A lot of it is built around frequencies between 432 and 900 Hz, made to resonate rather than just play. He co-hosted ZAOville with us in July and played his own set there.

LyonsDen plays 7th. LyonsDen Rez Muzik is a Native American, self-produced independent artist from the Onondaga Reservation, putting reggae roots, hip-hop, blues, R&B and EDM into 1 set with Rez storytelling underneath it. After the EP "Tales From SouthEnd" came his debut album, "The SouthEnd Kid". He won a Battle of the Bands to earn a spot at SXSW, joined the Beyond The Block Tour, and has collaborated with artists in the Netherlands and Belgium. He played ZAOville too.

Tom Fellenz plays 8th and closes the outdoor block. He is an acoustic guitar instrumentalist from the SF Bay Area, with original music shaped by 70s and 80s progressive rock, smooth jazz and acoustic folk. Listeners say his playing takes them on a journey, all from 1 guitar. His is the last thing you hear before the street clears.

3 artists from 3 places, on 1 stage in downtown Ellsworth.
