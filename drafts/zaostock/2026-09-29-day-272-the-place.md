ZAOstock is on the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine, and this is how to get there.

ZAOstock is a free music festival on Saturday, October 3, on the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine. 1 stage, 8 acts, from 12 to 6. At 6 the street clears, and Black Moon Public House next door hosts its own evening from 6. It is part of Art of Ellsworth, during Maine Craft Weekend.

Ellsworth is the crossroads of Downeast Maine and the gateway to Acadia National Park, with a downtown newly on the National Historic Register. For the day, Franklin Street closes to traffic and the parklet becomes the venue, under tent cover from Wallace Events. Black Moon Public House is right next door and open through the day.

By car it is about 40 minutes from Bangor, about 3 hours from Portland and about 5 hours from Boston. Route 1 and Route 1A run right through downtown. There is a public parking lot and free street parking near the parklet, so you do not need a shuttle.

If you are flying, Bangor International is the nearest major airport, about 40 minutes away, with a shuttle bus to Ellsworth twice a day. Hancock County-Bar Harbor is a smaller regional airport about 20 minutes away.

If you are staying over, book now. Early October is foliage season at the gateway to Acadia and rooms go fast. The 4 hotels on our visitor page are the Hampton Inn Ellsworth/Bar Harbor, the Comfort Inn Ellsworth - Bar Harbor, the Colonial Inn Ellsworth and the Hawthorn Extended Stay by Wyndham. The free Island Explorer shuttle connects Ellsworth-area hotels, Bar Harbor and Acadia through October 12.

Pack layers. Early October in Ellsworth usually runs around 58 to 62 degrees by day and about 44 at night, with roughly a 1 in 3 chance of rain on any given day. That is the typical pattern, not a forecast. We run rain or shine, so a packable rain layer is smart. The last set finishes near golden hour.

There are portable restrooms on site, including ADA-accessible units, and more within a short walk downtown.
