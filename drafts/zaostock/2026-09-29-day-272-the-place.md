ZAOstock is on the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine, and this is how to get there.

ZAOstock is a free music festival on Saturday, October 3, on the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine. 1 stage, 8 acts, from 12 to 6. At 6 the street clears, and Black Moon Public House next door hosts its own evening from 6, with North Creek. It is the first independent music event of the 9th Annual Art of Ellsworth, Maine Craft Weekend.

Ellsworth is the crossroads of Downeast Maine and the gateway to Acadia National Park, with a downtown newly on the National Historic Register. For the day, Franklin Street closes to traffic and the parklet becomes the venue. Black Moon Public House is right next door and open through the day.

By car it is about 40 minutes from Bangor, about 3 hours from Portland and about 5 hours from Boston. Route 1 and Route 1A run right through downtown. Franklin Street itself has no parking, because it is the venue. Park for free in the Franklin Street Parking Lot or in the lot at Ellsworth City Hall. Both are a short walk from the parklet.

If you are flying, Bangor International is the nearest major airport, about 40 minutes away, with a direct shuttle bus to Ellsworth twice a day. Hancock County-Bar Harbor is a smaller regional airport about 20 minutes away.

If you are staying over, book now. Early October is foliage season at the gateway to Acadia and rooms go fast. The 4 hotels on our visitor page are the Hampton Inn Ellsworth/Bar Harbor, the Comfort Inn Ellsworth - Bar Harbor, the Colonial Inn Ellsworth and the Hawthorn Extended Stay by Wyndham. The free Island Explorer shuttle connects Ellsworth-area hotels, Bar Harbor and Acadia through October 12.

There are portable restrooms on site, including ADA-accessible units, and more within a short walk downtown.

Everything above, plus where to eat and what to do with the rest of the weekend: [zaostock.com/ellsworth](https://zaostock.com/ellsworth)

RSVP is free at [ticket.zaostock.com](https://ticket.zaostock.com) so we know how many people to plan for. If you want to put something behind the day you can do that at [zaostock.com/tickets](https://zaostock.com/tickets).

See you on Franklin Street.
