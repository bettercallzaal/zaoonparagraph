year of the zabal day 268

Tom Fellenz closes ZAOstock, and he is coming from the SF Bay Area to do it with one guitar.

ZAOstock is a free music festival on Saturday, October 3, on the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine. 1 stage, 8 acts, from 12 to 6. At 6 the street clears, and Black Moon Public House next door hosts its own evening from 6. It is part of Art of Ellsworth, during Maine Craft Weekend.

Tom Fellenz is one of the 8. He plays 8th, which means he is the last thing you hear before the street clears.

He is a solo acoustic guitar instrumentalist, and there is no band behind that sentence. His own music is styled by 70s and 80s progressive rock, smooth jazz and acoustic folk, and he has played it across event stages, clubs, cafes, house concerts and online.

What people tell him about it is the part worth repeating, because it is a strange compliment to earn with six strings: that his playing is cinematic, and that it takes them somewhere. From introspective motivation to making your mark in the world, in his words, all from one guitar.

Eight acts in, after a rockabilly dance band, a stadium-sized trio, a jam band, flute and hand percussion, jazz piano, genre-defying hip hop and Native reggae, the day ends with one person and an acoustic guitar. That is a deliberate shape, and it is the right one.
