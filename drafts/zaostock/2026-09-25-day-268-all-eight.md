8 days until ZAOstock. Here are all 8 acts, in the order they play, in their own words.

## The 8

**The Crown Vics** open the day at noon. Their own billing is Maine's premier rock 'n' roll dance band, built on the bones of rockabilly, boogie woogie and honky tonk, twisted through classic rock and Americana. The line that tells you what the first hour will be like: no long solos, no slow fades, hardly a breath between numbers.

**OPEN X** play second. A bombastic, genre-blurring American rock power pop trio, in their words, with a cinematic sound, classical influences and shows built for a stadium. Three people making a dense, symphonic wall of sound, on a parklet, early in the afternoon.

**Grass Rug** are third. A rock band from Portland, Maine, formed in 2025, playing indie jam rock drawn from psychedelic 60s and 70s rock, 90s alternative and 2010s indie. Five of them, and their sets are improvised rather than fixed.

**Acadia Rising** are fourth, and they are from Ellsworth itself. Original flute and hand percussion music that blends global influences with the natural beauty of Maine, built on expressive melodies, dynamic rhythms and improvisation. The page calls it world rhythms, and it will sound like nothing else on the bill.

**Michael Anderson** plays fifth, from Bar Harbor. A voice and a keyboard, with roots in blues, soul and jazz, at home with jazz standards and popular hits alike. His own line: "I find it very important to connect with my audience."

**DCoop** is sixth, and he is coming from the DMV. Born from the roots of classic hip hop, blending it with reggae, rock, punk, tribal, country, EDM and R&B, and building much of his music on spiritual frequencies meant to resonate rather than just play.

**LyonsDen** is seventh, travelling from the Onondaga Reservation. A Native American, self-produced independent artist working where reggae roots, hip-hop grit, blues soul, R&B melodies and EDM energy collide. Island vibes and Rez storytelling in the same song, which is his own description and an accurate one.

**Tom Fellenz** closes, and he is coming from the SF Bay Area. A solo acoustic guitar instrumentalist whose originals are shaped by 70s and 80s progressive rock, smooth jazz and acoustic folk. People say his playing is cinematic, and it takes them somewhere, all from one guitar.

## What that list actually says

Five of the 8 have Maine ties, and three of them are travelling a long way to play a free show in a town of this size: DCoop from the DMV, LyonsDen from the Onondaga Reservation, Tom Fellenz from the SF Bay Area. Nobody is getting rich off this. They are coming because the bill is worth being on.

It also swings harder than most one-day bills: a rockabilly dance band, a stadium-sized power pop trio, an improvising jam band, flute and hand percussion, jazz piano and voice, genre-defying hip hop, Native reggae and hip-hop, and a solo acoustic guitarist. Eight acts, back to back, noon to 6.

[Every act has their own page.](https://zaostock.com/program)

## The day

ZAOstock is free, all ages, on Saturday, October 3, 2026, on the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine, from 12 to 6 pm Eastern. Rain or shine, under tent cover. At 6 the street clears and Black Moon Public House next door hosts its own evening.

There is no gate and no wristband. [RSVP for free](https://ticket.zaostock.com) so we know how much water, seating and shelter to plan for.
