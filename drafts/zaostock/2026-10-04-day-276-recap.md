year of the zabal day 276

ZAOstock happened. On Saturday Franklin Street closed, one stage went up on the parklet in downtown Ellsworth, and the music ran from noon to six. Thank you to everyone who came, played, worked and watched.

## The acts

Seven acts played the parklet stage: The Crown Vics, OPEN X, Grass Rug, Michael Anderson, DCoop, LyonsDen Rez Muzik and Tom Fellenz. Thank you to every one of them. You're the reason there was a day at all.

Then it moved next door. Black Moon Public House hosted the after-party, with North Creek, Treelock & HiDef, Sam Savage and Oven Baked Beats DJ Aquavantes, and it was awesome.

## The partners

None of this happens without the people who backed it. Thank you to Star 97.7, our radio partner, to Wallace Events for the equipment and the tent, to the City of Ellsworth for the parklet, to Heart of Ellsworth and Art of Ellsworth for making us part of Maine Craft Weekend, and to Black Moon Public House for taking the evening.

And thank you to the crew and the volunteers who made it run, and to everyone who watched from somewhere else or hosted a watch party.

## Watch it back

The whole day was streamed, and the replays are on [twitch.tv/zaofestivals](https://www.twitch.tv/zaofestivals). Photos and video will land on [the media page](https://zaostock.com/media) as they come in.

## Next year

We want to do this again. The date we're aiming for is Saturday, September 18, 2027. It could move, but put it in your calendar.

ZAOstock was free to attend. If you want to put something behind the next one, you can do it by card at [zaostock.com/tickets](https://zaostock.com/tickets) or through [Giveth](https://zaostock.com/giveth).

Thank you, Ellsworth.

- BetterCallZaal on behalf of the ZABAL Team
