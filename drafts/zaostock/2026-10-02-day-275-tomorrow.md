year of the zabal day 275

ZAOstock is tomorrow.

Saturday, October 3. The Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine. Music starts at noon.

It is free. It is all ages. There is no gate and no ticket to show, so if you did not RSVP, come anyway.

8 independent acts play 1 stage, back to back, until 6: The Crown Vics, OPEN X, Grass Rug, Acadia Rising, Michael Anderson, DCoop, LyonsDen, Tom Fellenz. At 6 the street clears and the ZAOstock after-party starts next door at Black Moon Public House, with a DJ.

There is a public lot and free street parking near the parklet. Bring layers and something for rain. Eat downtown, and buy something from a shop you walk past.

If you cannot be there, the stream plays at [zaostock.com/live](https://zaostock.com/live) from 12 to 6 Eastern. No sign-up.

This is the 4th ZAO Festivals chapter and the first one in Maine. It exists because a lot of people gave what they had: 10 partners, 8 acts, a crew of volunteers, and everyone who put something behind a day that costs nothing to attend. Thank you.

See you on Franklin Street.
