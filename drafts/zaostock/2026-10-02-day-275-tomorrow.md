year of the zabal day 275

ZAOstock is tomorrow.

Saturday, October 3. The Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine. Music starts at noon.

It is free. It is all ages. No ticket is checked at the parklet, so if you did not RSVP, come anyway.

8 independent acts play 1 stage, back to back, until 6: The Crown Vics, OPEN X, Grass Rug, Acadia Rising, Michael Anderson, DCoop, LyonsDen Rez Muzik, Tom Fellenz. At 6 the street clears. The ZAOstock after-party is at Black Moon Public House, hosted by Black Moon: doors from 6, and music from 7 to 10 with North Creek, Treelock & HiDef, Sam Savage and Oven Baked Beats DJ Aquavantes.

Franklin Street itself is the venue, so park for free in the Franklin Street Parking Lot or at Ellsworth City Hall, both a short walk away. Eat downtown, and buy something from a shop you walk past.

If you cannot be there, the stream plays at [zaostock.com/live](https://zaostock.com/live) from 12 to 6 Eastern. No sign-up.

This is the 4th ZAO Festivals chapter and the first one in Maine. It exists because a lot of people gave what they had: our partners, 8 acts, a crew of volunteers, and everyone who put something behind a day that costs nothing to attend. Thank you.

See you on Franklin Street.

[zaostock.com/program](https://zaostock.com/program)
