OPEN X are playing ZAOstock.

ZAOstock is a free music festival on Saturday, October 3, on the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine. 1 stage, 8 acts, from 12 to 6. At 6 the street clears, and Black Moon Public House next door hosts its own evening from 6, with North Creek. It is the first independent music event of the 9th Annual Art of Ellsworth, Maine Craft Weekend.

OPEN X are one of the 8. They are from Down East Maine.

They describe themselves as a bombastic, genre-blurring American rock power pop trio, with a cinematic sound, classical influences and live shows built for a stadium. The roots are alternative rock, and from there they blend in progressive rock, heavy metal and modern pop.

What that sounds like is thick, heavy guitar riffs set against delicate vocal melodies. A big part of their identity is Ryan Miller's dramatic, soaring and emotional vocal delivery. There are only 3 of them, and they produce a dense, symphonic wall of sound, anchored by driving basslines and precise, hard-hitting percussion.

A 3-piece that plays like a stadium band, on a parklet in downtown Ellsworth, early in the day. It will carry down the street.

They play 2nd, right after The Crown Vics, so come at noon and you get both.

Their page: [zaostock.com/artist/open-x](https://zaostock.com/artist/open-x)

Their site: [openxofficial.com](https://openxofficial.com)

The running order: [zaostock.com/program](https://zaostock.com/program)

RSVP is free at [ticket.zaostock.com](https://ticket.zaostock.com) so we know how many people to plan for. If you want to put something behind the day you can do that at [zaostock.com/tickets](https://zaostock.com/tickets).

See you on Franklin Street.
