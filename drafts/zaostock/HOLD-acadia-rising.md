Acadia Rising is playing ZAOstock.

ZAOstock is a free music festival on Saturday, October 3, on the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine. 1 stage, 8 acts, from 12 to 6. At 6 the street clears, and Black Moon Public House next door hosts its own evening from 6. It is part of Art of Ellsworth, during Maine Craft Weekend.

Acadia Rising is one of the 8, and the hometown act. The project is based in Ellsworth.

The music is original flute and hand percussion. It blends global musical influences with the natural beauty of Maine: expressive flute melodies, dynamic rhythms and improvisation, with each piece inspired by landscapes, cultures and traditions from around the world. The performance is built as a musical journey, one that celebrates rhythm, connection and the universal language of music.

Nothing else on the bill sounds like it, which is the point of putting 8 independent acts on 1 stage. Between a jam band and a solo piano, Franklin Street gets flute and hand percussion.

Acadia Rising plays 4th.
