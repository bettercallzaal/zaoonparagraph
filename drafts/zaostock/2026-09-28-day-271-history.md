year of the zabal day 271

ZAOstock is the 4th ZAO Festivals event. Before a street in Ellsworth, Maine there was a room in New York, a night in Miami and a pool in Maryland. Today we want to tell you how we got here.

## Where it started: ZAO-PALOOZA, New York, April 2024

The first time a lot of us were in the same room. ZAO-PALOOZA ran during NFT NYC with 12 artists on the bill, organised by volunteers in 6 weeks. 6 of the 12 had made music onchain before and 6 were new to it. People who had only known each other as names and profile pictures met for real, and the thing we had been building online turned into a night of live music.

There was no guest book. You signed a link of a paper chain and added it to the rest.

It broke even. For a first event, run by a community with no venue of its own, that was a win, and it told us to do it again.

## Going bigger: ZAO-CHELLA, Miami, December 2024

On December 6, 2024, in the middle of Art Basel week, ZAO-CHELLA took over a space in Wynwood, Miami. Doors opened at 4 pm for networking. At 6, WaveWarZ played the first battle it had ever run live on a stage, a rematch of one fought online. From 7 until the 11 pm close, 16+ musicians played, with 100+ visual artists, AR art and trading cards in the room.

Some of the best of it happened off the stage. Artists stayed in one house that week and recorded a song together, which is still unreleased. And 3 people who could not make the trip wrote and recorded a ZAO-CHELLA song of their own in about a day, from wherever they were.

Music, art and a live battle on the same stage, in a city packed with events. That is the energy we are carrying to Maine.

## Between the big nights: COC Concertz and WaveWarZ

When we were not in the same city, COC Concertz kept the music going online, with concerts in the metaverse streamed live, so nobody needed a plane ticket to be part of it. 2025 went to WaveWarZ. It was also the year Zaal moved to Maine, and started looking at what could happen where he lives.

## The third one: ZAOville, Maryland, July 2026

On July 25, 2026, ZAOville was a pool party in Laurel, Maryland, co-hosted with DCoop, founder of The VEC. It was free. There was an open mic, live independent sets and a DJ night swim to close. DCoop and LyonsDen both played it, and both are on the ZAOstock bill.

## Where it is going: ZAOstock, October 3

All of that leads here. ZAOstock is a free music festival on Saturday, October 3, on the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine. 1 stage, 8 acts, from 12 to 6, no ticket and no wristband for the street. It is the first independent music event of the 9th Annual Art of Ellsworth, Maine Craft Weekend.

Here is who you will hear, in running order. The Crown Vics open the day, a rock n roll dance band. OPEN X bring power pop rock. Grass Rug bring indie jam rock from Portland, Maine. Acadia Rising bring world rhythms, flute and hand percussion, from Ellsworth itself. Michael Anderson plays solo piano, blues, soul and jazz, from Bar Harbor. DCoop brings hip-hop that pulls from reggae, rock, punk, tribal, country, EDM and R&B. LyonsDen brings reggae roots, hip-hop, blues, R&B and EDM. Tom Fellenz closes the outdoor block on solo instrumental acoustic guitar.

At 6 the street clears, and Black Moon Public House next door hosts its own evening from 6, with North Creek.

Not in Maine? The whole outdoor block streams at [zaostock.com/live](https://zaostock.com/live), noon to 6 Eastern on October 3.

## Why it is on Franklin Street

We put this on a real street on purpose. Eat at the places around you, drink at Black Moon, buy something from the shop you walk past. We are measuring what October 3 does for this block, and that number only exists if you show up and make it.
