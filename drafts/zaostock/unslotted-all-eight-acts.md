8 independent acts play ZAOstock, back to back on 1 stage. This is every one of them, in the order they play.

ZAOstock is a free music festival on Saturday, October 3, on the Franklin Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, Maine. 1 stage, 8 acts, from 12 to 6. At 6 the street clears, and Black Moon Public House next door hosts its own evening from 6, with North Creek. It is the first independent music event of the 9th Annual Art of Ellsworth, Maine Craft Weekend.

5 of the 8 are Maine acts. They play first.

The Crown Vics open the day. They are from the Ellsworth and Bangor area and bill themselves as Maine's premier rock 'n' roll dance band, built on the bones of rockabilly, boogie woogie and honky tonk. No long solos, no slow fades, hardly a breath between numbers. [Their page.](https://zaostock.com/artist/the-crown-vics)

OPEN X play 2nd. They are a rock power pop trio from Down East Maine, rooted in alternative rock, with thick, heavy guitar riffs set against delicate vocal melodies and Ryan Miller's soaring voice on top. [Their page.](https://zaostock.com/artist/open-x)

Grass Rug play 3rd. They are a 5-piece from Portland, Maine, formed in 2025, known for an indie jam-rock sound and for live sets that are improvised on the night. [Their page.](https://zaostock.com/artist/grass-rug)

Acadia Rising plays 4th, and is the hometown act, based in Ellsworth. The music is original flute and hand percussion, blending global influences with the natural beauty of Maine. [The page.](https://zaostock.com/artist/acadia-rising)

Michael Anderson plays 5th, solo piano. He lives in Bar Harbor, his roots are in blues, soul and jazz, and he pairs an outstanding voice with clever keyboard playing. [His page.](https://zaostock.com/artist/michael-anderson)

Then come the ZAO musicians, the 3 who travel in. They headline, and they are the last 3 acts of the day.

DCoop plays 6th. He is a hip-hop artist from the DMV, and his music starts in classic hip-hop and pulls from reggae, rock, punk, tribal, country, EDM and R&B. [His page.](https://zaostock.com/artist/dcoop)

LyonsDen plays 7th. LyonsDen Rez Muzik is a Native American, self-produced independent artist from the Onondaga Reservation, putting reggae roots, hip-hop, blues, R&B and EDM into 1 set with Rez storytelling underneath it. [His page.](https://zaostock.com/artist/lyonsden)

Tom Fellenz plays 8th and closes the outdoor block. He is an acoustic guitar instrumentalist from the SF Bay Area, with original music shaped by 70s and 80s progressive rock, smooth jazz and acoustic folk. [His page.](https://zaostock.com/artist/tom-fellenz)

Music starts at noon. We are not publishing set times, so come at noon and stay.

The running order: [zaostock.com/program](https://zaostock.com/program)

Not in Maine? The outdoor block streams at [zaostock.com/live](https://zaostock.com/live) from 12 to 6 Eastern, with no sign-up.

RSVP is free at [ticket.zaostock.com](https://ticket.zaostock.com) so we know how many people to plan for. If you want to put something behind the day you can do that at [zaostock.com/tickets](https://zaostock.com/tickets).

See you on Franklin Street.
