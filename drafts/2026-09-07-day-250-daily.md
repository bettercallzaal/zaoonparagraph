year of the zabal day 250

got on the wrong train twice in one day

not because the route was hard

because i had nowhere i had to be

i didnt plan this weekend at all

no schedule no list of who i was seeing

super open on purpose

landed thursday with the conference already over

nft nyc ran the first through the third and i got in as it ended

turns out the week wasnt over at all

art nyc was running the whole time

thirty plus events i didnt know existed

started at the canal street show at lume studios

beyond the veil

seventy plus artists in one building

live art battle and judasaca and alyssa stevens took it

caught attabotty at the end of the night

ran into desultor who i hadnt seen in a min

five minutes down the road christiano covino was running underground sound at bowery palace

doors at eleven free until four in the morning

that room was full of onchain music people

mr mcfly stu nessy mojo the ghost dr bruce beamer jay connects

two rooms five minutes apart

met more people in a night than a month of posting

here is the part that got me

we minted a card series for the zao palooza artists back in 2024

attabotty christiano mojo the ghost ne$$ytherilla

four of them were in those two rooms

two years later same city

christiano wasnt just there he was running it

and i didnt clock a single bit of it while i was standing there

thats the cost of showing up blind

ten minutes of prep and i say something completely different to all four of them

friday i went to the solana offices alone

great space

everybody heads down working

a coworking floor is not a networking room

walking into one by yourself is walking into a library

got nothing for a while and i knew it

then judasaca walked in

juan salazar works as judasaca

colombian artist

traditional and digital and ar

he took the battle the night before

we had been trying to link up forever and there he was

whole day turned over in about ten seconds

did an interview with him on the roof

best backdrop i have had for one of these

no version of that weekend where i schedule that

same room same afternoon

alone got me nothing

recognized got me the best thing i did all year

after that we went to meet a friend of his and yuzapata who i never caught this trip

heard a band play live in a studio

no stage no lineup

just playing for whoever was standing there

then meet me at the buzzer for shanelle julia rosita closing out homage to new york

finished at broome street gallery night at cycol

cat russell handed me a magic the gathering deck box

cat cuts paper for a living

just gave it to me

in a gallery on a friday for nothing

closed the night at candys running our nightly wavewarz quick battles

all weekend i kept turning corners into work i already had a connection to

one love art dao had artists up across the city

all of that work is jen

we built with her at zao chella

walking around nyc seeing her artists on walls in rooms i wandered into with no plan

that was a blast

and thats the honest part

those rooms were warm because of 2024

not because of anything i did in september

saturday was the one day i actually had names in my head

lui jango uu stu kwan maybe mr mcfly

none of it happened

jango wasnt around

he is the reason any of this exists

new york musician i met in college

i was in his city four days and didnt see him

no schedule would have fixed that one

still the one i feel

what i got instead was a full day with candytoybox

just the two of us

ended up at pier 57 talking wavewarz over the hudson

easiest day of the trip

because i spent it with somebody i didnt have to arrange to meet

our first festival was zao palooza at nft nyc in april 2024

six people organized it

never met in person until the day of

saw the venue for the first time that same day

six weeks twelve artists broke even

i have told that for two years as proof you can wing it

i dont think thats what it proves

twelve artists trusted six strangers and it worked once

thats not a plan thats a thing you get away with

so the ledger

spontaneity got me four of my own artists in a room i walked into blind

a roof interview i could not have scheduled

a deck box from somebody who owed me nothing

jens work on walls all over the city

and the easiest day of the trip

it cost me stu kwan lui yuzapata

an hour on a quiet floor

forty minutes on two wrong platforms

and standing in a room with four of my own artists without knowing it

i would take that trade again

but it is a trade

and the good half wasnt free either

every warm room i walked into was warm because somebody did the work years earlier

you cant schedule the moment

you can leave space for it

and you can spend years being somebody the room already knows

first one is cheap

second one is the whole job
