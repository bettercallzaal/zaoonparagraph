## Mechanics, so the embeds actually work

1. Zaal posts each one on X with its photo attached.
2. He sends me the post URLs.
3. The URLs go into the article as **real embed blocks**, not plain links. The `twitter`
   node's `tweetData` blob is built by the editor on paste and must not be hand-authored -
   use the `+` toolbar Embed block, or hand the bare URL to the Paragraph chat with exact
   placement.
